# Gameplay Overview

Past Halo titles have several systems that make it feel like a sandbox even though its mostly a straightforward FPS title. We aim to expand on that core Halo-feel of being a mix of FPS and sandbox elements while expanding to incorporate other strategic elements, which include base building, fortifying, and commanding squads of units. We DONT want to overwhelm the player with these various systems and largely strive to keep the player in a First-person perspective for the majority of a game instance, but want to give the player the opportunity to feel more connected with the various sandbox elements of them game rather than them being solely controlled by the game designer and randomness.
