# Gameplay Overview

Past Halo titles have several systems that make it feel like a sandbox even though its mostly a straightforward FPS title. This game aims to expand on that core Halo-feel of being a mix of FPS and sandbox elements while expanding to incorporate other strategic elements, which include base building, fortifying, and commanding squads of units. While this game will aim to incorporate various strategy elements like base building, fortifying, and commanding units, we will aim to keep the game flow simplistic.
